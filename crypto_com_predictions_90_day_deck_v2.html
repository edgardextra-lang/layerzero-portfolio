B2B platform hypothesis
09 / 10
Strategic expansion bet

Predictions can become the embedded market layer for trusted fan ecosystems.

Hypothesis: Crypto.com can sell a regulated, white-label or embedded predictions platform to organizations like FIFA, NHL, NBA, Formula 1, UFC, and major global brands — turning existing fan attention into new revenue for partners and Crypto.com.

Fan engagement
Embedded liquidity
Trusted institutions
First CDNA exchange
Trusted / regulated rails
Why this could work
  • Fans already trust leagues, teams, and major brands more than new prediction-market venues.
  • Those partners own demand, distribution, moments, data, and large recurring event calendars.
  • Many fans understand sports opinions, but are not yet educated on prediction markets.
  • Crypto.com can bring regulated exchange infrastructure, market operations, custody, payments, and settlement — positioned as the first CDNA exchange in the user's framing: trusted, regulated, and already distribution-ready.
